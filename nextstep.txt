2. Step 2.1 ('analysis/statistics.py'):
   - get_numeric_summary(df): Mean, std, var, min, 25%, 50%, 75%, max, IQR, skewness, kurtosis.
   - get_categorical_summary(df): Unique count, mode value, mode frequency %, missing counts/%.
   - get_distribution_stats(series): Skewness/kurtosis heuristics and distribution shape labels.

3. Step 2.2 ('analysis/missing_values.py'):
   - get_missing_summary(df): Column breakdown sorted by missing % descending.
   - get_missingness_overview(df): KPI metrics (total cells, missing cells, overall %, cols with missing).
   - impute_missing_values(df, strategy): Clean/fill missing values supporting mean, median, mode, drop_rows, drop_cols.

4. Step 2.3 ('analysis/duplicates.py'):
   - get_duplicate_summary(df, subset): Summarizes duplicate rows (count, percentage, has_duplicates flag).
   - drop_duplicates_clean(df, subset, keep): Safe deduplication on DataFrame copy with reset index.

5. Step 2.4 ('analysis/correlations.py'):
   - calculate_correlation_matrix(df, method): Safe numeric correlation supporting pearson, spearman, kendall.
   - get_top_correlations(df, threshold, top_n, method): Extracts unique upper-triangle pairs, rates direction/strength.
   - get_target_correlations(df, target_column, method): Computes correlation of all numerical features against a target.
   - get_correlation_overview(df, threshold): Returns high-level metrics and multicollinearity alerts.

6. Step 2.5 ('analysis/outliers.py'):
   - detect_outliers_iqr(series, factor=1.5): Outlier detection via Tukey's IQR rule with full metadata.
   - detect_outliers_zscore(series, threshold=3.0): Outlier detection via Z-Score with zero-variance protection.
   - get_outliers_summary(df, method='iqr'): Tabular summary across all numeric columns sorted by outlier count.
   - cap_outliers(df, column, factor=1.5): Winsorization clamping without row deletion.
   - drop_outliers(df, columns=None, factor=1.5): Outlier row removal across numeric columns.

7. Step 2.6 ('analysis/profiling.py') [COMPLETED TODAY]:
   - calculate_health_score(df): Composite quality score (0 - 100), letter grade (A-F), and bounded penalty deductions.
   - generate_dataset_audit(df): Comprehensive master diagnostic audit aggregating shape, memory, column types, missingness, duplicates, outliers, health score, and actionable text warnings.

8. Step 2.7 (Exporting, Testing & Verification) [COMPLETED TODAY]:
